Help & Shortcuts

Space: Play / Pause

Left / Right Arrow: Skip forward / backward

Click & Drag background: Marquee select notes

Shift + Click: Toggle selection on individual notes

C: Chordify selected notes (align them perfectly)

Ctrl/Cmd + C: Copy selected notes

Ctrl/Cmd + V: Paste copied notes at mouse cursor

Backspace / Delete: Delete selected notes

B: Toggle Phrase Break marker at playhead

Ctrl/Cmd + Z: Undo

Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z / Cmd + Y: Redo
